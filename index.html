FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What accounting services does ASCO provide?

ASCO offers a comprehensive range of accounting services including bookkeeping, financial reporting, VAT compliance, corporate tax advisory, internal auditing, inventory management, business valuation, accounting system implementation, management consultancy, and CFO services.

Do you work with businesses outside of Dubai?

Yes, we serve businesses across all Emirates of the UAE. While our office is in Dubai, we work with clients in Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, Ajman, Fujairah, Ras Al Khaimah, and Umm Al Quwain through a combination of in-person meetings and digital collaboration tools.

Which industries do you work with?

We support education, healthcare, logistics, retail, hospitality, manufacturing, construction, and media & advertising businesses — from startups and SMEs to established companies.

How does working with ASCO start?

With a free consultation. We listen to understand your business and goals, review your current financial position, recommend clear next steps, and then provide ongoing support to keep you compliant and informed.
